Built for Absolute Privacy

We believe your conversations are yours alone. Here's how we protect them:

Zero-Knowledge E2EE

Every message is encrypted locally using AES-GCM and RSA-OAEP before leaving your device. Our servers only route encrypted blobs and never possess the keys to decrypt your data.

No Identity Tracking

Unlike other platforms, Ghost Link requires no email, phone number, or social logins. You operate solely under a randomly generated Ghost ID, making you completely anonymous.

Screenshot Protection

Our client detects screenshot attempts via OS level shortcuts or focus loss. If triggered, your chat is instantly hidden and your peer is securely alerted.

        Support & FAQ

        General FAQs

        Is Ghost Link truly zero-knowledge? +

        Yes. All chat channels are completely encrypted end-to-end on the client side using secure modern RSA-OAEP/AES-GCM handshakes. The servers only act as a relay and never see the key exchanges or decrypted text content.

        How do screenshot alerts work? +

        Ghost Link listens to platform keystrokes and document blur handshakes. When a screenshot is taken, a notification is silently and automatically sent to your peer room instantly.

        What happens if I change my username? +

        Your old username is instantly released so other users can register it. All active direct chats are mapped to the new username bundle immediately.
